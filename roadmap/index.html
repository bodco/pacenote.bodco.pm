Now, next, later

Now

Better briefs and tips

Every call is reviewed after it ends: which tips helped, which were noise, what was missed. The prompts change from that.

Next

Two-factor sign-in

A code from any authenticator app, backup codes, and an organization policy that makes it required. Touch ID or a passcode when you sign in.

Service emails

Emails for invites, a sign-in from a new device and getting back into two-factor sign-in.

Call analysis against a standard

Presets by team type (Sales, PM, Recruiting and more), a scorecard for each kind of call, a score after the call and an overview for team leads.

Prompts you can see

The prompts behind your brief, preparation and notes in Settings, with your own additions, and which prompt wrote a note.

Delete a call everywhere

Remove a call you did not mean to record from Pacenote, Notion, your Mac and HubSpot, and pick where it goes from.

Security hardening

Fixes from our own security review of the app and the update channel, before the team version grows.

Signed and notarized by Apple

No more "Open Anyway" on first launch and no Keychain prompt after an update.

Later

Live tips without your own key

Tip hours included in a plan, with top-ups. Your own AI key stays an option on every plan.

Paid plans

Billing after the beta, with 30 days' notice to everyone in the beta. Solo stays free.

On your phone

A web app for the phone: calendar, Meeting Sheet, notes after the call, tasks.

The download link by email

On a phone, the site sends you the link itself once our service mail is on. Until then it opens your mail app with the link ready.

What shipped

  1. 23 Nov · 0.27Call language and HubSpot. Notes, Summary, the follow-up, the Meeting Sheet, the brief and Notion follow the language of the call, and a call in two or three languages can be regenerated in another one. A Transcript button and a call language chip in the tips window. Update HubSpot logs the call, its people, deal fields, the meeting notes and your follow-up email, each with Undo, and your HubSpot tasks show in the Tracker. Downloads you can cancel. Gemini now through an API key.
  2. 20 Nov · 0.26.2-0.26.4Calls in folders, speakers you can fix. A folder for every call, and older calls move into theirs on their own. Fix who said what after the call, merge speakers and remember a voice, then Rewrite notes with the right names. Model lists come from your AI provider. Sign in to the web console with your email and password. Fixes from the beta.
  3. 19 Nov · 0.26-0.26.1Connect with Notion. Connect Notion in one click and keep a local copy of it without Control Tower. Notices inside Pacenote instead of pop-up alerts, questions right where you clicked, and what happened at the top of the Summary.
  4. 18-19 Nov · 0.24-0.25New screen layouts. Home, the call pages, the Meeting Sheet steps, Tracker, Past calls, Statistics and Settings laid out anew. Roadbook and Broadsheet redrawn, who decided next to each decision, tips as key, detail and source, a compact tip card under the camera. Setup with no Terminal or browser, and updates in one click.
  5. 18 Nov · 0.23Three looks. Classic, Roadbook and Broadsheet, each light or dark, in Settings > General > Appearance. The new Pacenote mark and motion, the tips window shows its state in the style, and a window and a banner tell you when a new version is out. On the site: plans on Pricing and a Download page.
  6. 17-18 Nov · 0.20-0.22Team calls, sales, email sign-in. The Team tab for team leads, more sales methods and deal blocks, a speech writer for a deck, and sign-in with email and password next to Google. An account is now required.
  7. 17 Nov · 0.19Plans in the app. Compare plans and switch an organization's plan in Settings, start a team from Solo, a trial on every plan, your own use for the last 30 days, a Console page for everyone signed in. Product analytics you can turn off: screens, errors by code and speed, never what is said in a call. A recorded call stops by itself when the meeting ends.
  8. 16-17 Nov · 0.17Organizations. Invites, teams with a lead, private calls, organization projects with shared decisions, tasks and risks, the admin console inside the app.
  9. 15-16 Nov · 0.14-0.16A calmer app. New Get Ready, Settings in groups with search, onboarding where you pick what to install, removable add-ons, automatic updates and "What's new".
  10. 15 Nov · 0.11-0.13Account and sync. Sign in to Pacenote, recordings and transcripts in your Google Drive, call notes synced per project (off by default), one list of decisions, tasks and risks everywhere.
  11. 11-14 Nov · 0.10Meeting Sheet. One preparation for every call, Quick and Expert, sources from folders and files, readiness check. The co-driver: a prompter, answers to anyone's questions, a fallback chain across AI providers.
  12. 2-9 Nov · 0.9Beyond Claude. Google accounts, OpenAI and Gemini, call recording to Google Drive, tip ratings that teach the app, Start call opens the meeting in the right place.
  13. 28 Oct-1 Nov · 0.8Pacenote. The new name and look, the menu bar panel, presentation and rehearsal modes, notes and tasks after the call.
  14. 22-28 Oct · 0.4-0.7The Mac app. A tips window of its own, settings in the Keychain, projects, notes in Notion, voices, interview coach mode.
  15. 10-21 OctThe skill. Live tips as a Cowork skill: a tips window with a question field, interview mode, cards on the fly.

Every change, release by release, is in the changelog. Dates and order of planned items can change: this page shows the plan as it stands today.
