Pacenote vs Fireflies vs Fathom

Fireflies and Fathom record the meeting; Pacenote prepares you from your own project and helps you while you talk, with the audio never leaving your Mac.

At a glance

PickPacenote if

  • You want live help from your own project prep.
  • Audio must stay on your Mac, with no bot in the call.
  • You present, rehearse, or run calls back to back.
  • You run sales calls with BANT, SPICED or MEDDIC and keep deals in HubSpot.

PickFireflies if

  • A whole team needs one searchable meeting archive.
  • You need CRM sync, SSO and admin controls.
  • You record in many languages and on Windows.

PickFathom if

  • You want free recordings and summaries.
  • You sell and want video clips and CRM notes.

They work together: Pacenote while you talk, Fireflies or Fathom as the archive after.

Price

Pacenote

$0 app, no seats

Preparation and notes from your Claude plan. On an API key a typical call is about $1.05 for 30 min and $1.15 for an hour (Sonnet brief, Haiku tips, measured), with a monthly limit you set.

Fireflies

$10-18 a seat a month

Free: 400 min storage per team, 20 AI credits, 2 h per recording. Pro $10 / $18, Business $19 / $29, Enterprise $39 (yearly / monthly).

Fathom

$0-20 a seat a month

Free: unlimited recordings, advanced summaries for 5 calls a month. Premium $16 / $20, Team $15 / $19 (2 seats min), Business $25 / $34.

Pacenote on an API key: a preparation is about $0.8 with Sonnet; live tips on Haiku about $0.05 per 14 min. Details in the documentation.

What users say

From G2, Capterra, Trustpilot and independent tests in 2025-2026 (links below).

Fireflies

  • Praised: time-saving summaries and action items; search across past meetings (AskFred); HubSpot sync.
  • Criticised: speakers mixed up on busy calls; the bot joins by default; AI credits run out fast.
  • Live Assist and Meeting Prep: few reports yet; testers found the in-call chat fiddly.

Fathom

  • Praised: accurate summaries, one-click follow-up emails, a generous free plan.
  • Criticised: the bot sometimes fails to join, or joins twice; slow email-only support.
  • Languages: summaries in 7 languages; names and non-English terms come out wrong.

Requirements

  • Mac with Apple Silicon. Setup downloads about 2.5 GB of speech and search models.
  • CRM: HubSpot, with your own service key in the macOS Keychain.
  • Audio and text, no video. Summaries go to Notion today; Google Docs and Word are next.
  • Personal tool; team version designed. Read the design.
