Your co-driver on calls

Preps you from your own project files, shows the fact or phrase you need the second you need it, and writes the notes and the follow-up when you hang up.

See it on a call

Why Pacenote

🎯 Facts or silence

A tip carries a number, a date, a name or a ready sentence. Or it is not shown.

⚡ Works without prep

Start anyway: tips when you are addressed, HELP NOW, project search.

🧠 Knows your project

Your folders, your Notion, full notes of every earlier call.

💳 Pay per call, not per seat

On your Claude plan, or about $1.05 a 30-min call on a key, brief included.

Private where it matters, smart where it counts

On your Mac, free

  • Whisper: speech to text, audio stays local.
  • Instant search: facts and files by meaning, under a second.
  • Voices: who is speaking, remembered per project.
  • Local model (optional): preparation and notes with no cloud.

Claude, your plan or key

  • Live tips, HELP NOW, answers to your questions.
  • Briefs, speeches and rehearsal questions.
  • The sales brief, with a web search for the company.
  • The Notion page and the follow-up draft.

Sales calls

🧭 Pick a method

General sales by default, or BANT, SPICED, MEDDIC. On top of any call type.

📊 Deal tab

Each criterion unknown, partly or known, with what was said. "BANT 2/4" in the header.

💡 Sales tips

Objection, qualify, pain, next step, competitor, buying signal. Plus your answer cards.

🔁 HubSpot on your click

A snapshot before the call. After it: tick, Apply, Undo for 8 s.

The brief adds the company (a web search with its name or domain only), likely objections and questions to ask. After the call: a Sales section and a scorecard.

Two ways to run it

For most people

💻 Pacenote for Mac

A menu bar app on your Claude plan or API key. Setup installs the speech models for you.

Download for Mac (.dmg)

First launch: System Settings > Privacy & Security > Open Anyway. SHA-256 checksums.

For Claude Cowork users

🧩 Pacenote skill

The same tips and rules inside your Cowork chat, on your Claude plan. About 15 minutes to set up.

Download the skill (.skill)

Add it in Cowork under Skills, then follow the install steps.

Requirements: Mac with Apple Silicon · macOS 14.4+ · about 2.5 GB of local models · a Claude plan or API key · Notion and HubSpot optional. Next: summaries to Google Docs or Word, Pacenote for teams.
