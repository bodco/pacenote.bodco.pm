Switching from…

Fireflies

Transcript, recording, notes, action items and a CRM update are in Pacenote too, on your Mac. On top: a brief before the call and answers while you talk. Pacenote has no shared team workspace: if your team lives in Fireflies, keep it for that.

Fathom

Fathom’s free recorder is good. Pacenote’s is free too and never leaves your Mac: transcript, screen, notes. Plus the part Fathom skips: your files before the call and answers during it. If your team shares recordings in Fathom, keep it for that.

Granola

Pacenote writes the notes after the call too, and the audio is transcribed on your Mac. The difference is before and during: a brief from your project files, and the number, date or sentence you need under your camera while you talk.

Zoom, Teams, Meet AI

Built-in AI lives in one platform. Pacenote works the same in Zoom, Meet, Teams and Slack, knows your project, and helps during the call. From Google Meet it imports the participants and the Meet transcript.

Just notes

Keep writing if you like. Pacenote reads your doc before the call, along with Notion, Google Docs and earlier calls, shows the line you need during the call, and writes the notes to Notion, Google Docs, Word or Markdown after it.

Your calls, your language.

For calls in a language that is not your first.

Before you install

How fast is a tip, and is it right?

Fact cards from your prep show in 1-2 s, or nothing shows at all. In this beta, your tip ratings and corrections shape later calls.

How much setup before it is useful?

A calendar invite and one doc or folder are enough to start. The Setup window downloads about 2.5 GB and needs no admin password.

Is it $0, or do I need an API key?

On your plan, tips need a small API key: about $1 a month. Fully on your Mac, with simpler cards, it costs $0.

Can I trust an unsigned beta?

It is not notarized yet, so first launch needs Open Anyway in Privacy & Security. The code is open (MIT), and you can check the download’s SHA-256.

What does it do with my Google account?

Google is optional and read-only, except the Doc you export. Calendars also work through macOS; sign-in warns "unverified app" while Google reviews the app.

Where does my call text go?

Audio stays on your Mac, and only text reaches the AI provider you pick, under your own account or API terms. The author gets nothing unless you switch on usage counts.

What do I tell people on the call?

Tell them you keep a transcript and use an AI assistant. Get consent where the law or your employer requires it.

Do I have to drop my note taker?

You may not need it: transcript, recording and notes are in Pacenote too, free on your Mac. Keep it if your team shares recordings there.

Which call apps and Macs does it work with?

Any call app on your Mac, such as Zoom, Meet, Teams or Slack. It listens to the Mac’s sound and needs Apple Silicon with macOS 14.4+.
