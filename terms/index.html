To the extent the law allows, the author is not liable for any loss or damage from using it, including missed or wrong tips, transcription errors, lost transcripts, costs of the AI services it calls, or decisions made during or after a call.

Your responsibility

  • Consent of the people on the call. You are responsible for telling participants that you transcribe the call and use an AI assistant, and for getting their consent where the law, your employer or your client requires it. Do not use Pacenote where recording or transcription is not allowed. Pacenote’s consent record only notes that a message was said or copied, not that anyone agreed.
  • AI can be wrong. Tips, summaries and transcripts can mishear names, numbers and terms or miss context. Check them before you say, send or decide anything on their basis.
  • Your accounts and costs. Pacenote uses your own Claude, ChatGPT or Gemini key or account (or another provider’s key), your Notion and your Mac. Their terms, limits and costs apply to you. Set the call and monthly budgets in Settings. A Gemini account runs through Google’s Antigravity CLI, whose terms forbid use through third-party software and let Google use and review the conversations: choosing it is your decision and your risk, including the suspension of your Google account.
  • Your data and your employer’s rules. Make sure that processing work calls and project files with AI is allowed by your employer and your clients. Keep transcripts and voice prints out of public places.
  • Nothing is sent for you. Pacenote prepares notes and drafts; sending anything to a client is always your own action.
  • Fair use of the site. Do not attack or overload this website.

Pacenote account and server (0.11.0, optional)

The Pacenote account is optional: without it the app works as before. If you sign in in Settings > Account:

  • Beta, no uptime promise. The server is in beta and can be down or change. Keep your own copies: your calls stay on your Mac and your recordings in your Drive.
  • What you send is your responsibility. You, or the organization you use Pacenote for, are responsible for what you send to the server, including confidential and personal information in summaries, Meeting Sheets and shared project cards, project state and file descriptions, and for having the right to send it. If you use Pacenote for an employer or a client, follow their rules on where their information may be stored.
  • Organizations. An organization’s owner decides who sees what within it and how long data is kept.
  • Plans in the beta (0.19.0). During the beta every plan is free. An owner or admin of an organization can switch its plan, and a person on Solo can start a team organization; a switch applies at once. Planned prices are shown struck through and may change; we will tell you 30 days before any plan costs money. A custom plan is changed by Pacenote on request.
  • Fair use. Do not store on the server what you are not allowed to store, and do not try to reach other people’s data; I may suspend an account that does.
  • Leaving. You can delete your account at any time in Settings > Account. If the service ends, you get at least 30 days to export.

How the server handles your data is in the privacy policy.
